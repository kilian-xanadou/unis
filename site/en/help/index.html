Help: shipping, returns and questions

Delivered to your letterbox

Shipping

Delivery across the European Union in 2 to 4 working days. €3.90, free from €30. Thongs arrive in a kraft pouch that fits through the letterbox, Bo in his gift box.

Returns and exchanges

You have 14 days to return an item. For hygiene reasons, a thong can only be returned if it hasn't been worn and the hygiene seal is intact.

Prices and payment

Prices include your country's VAT. Any discount shows the lowest price of the last 30 days next to it.

Frequently asked questions

Updated on 16 November 2026

The practical bit

How do I choose my size?

Measure your hips at the widest point, then check the size guide, from XS (82–88 cm) to 4XL (128–136 cm). Between two sizes? Go for the bigger one.

What's the difference between Flat and Pouch?

Flat has a smooth, close-fitting front. Pouch has a shaped front that gives more room. Both have the same soft waistband.

How do I wash my thong?

Machine wash at 30 °C, inside out, no fabric softener. Air dry it: the cotton keeps its shape for longer.

Orders and shipping

How much is shipping?

€3.90 anywhere in the EU, free from €30. Delivery in 2 to 4 working days, straight through your letterbox.

Can I return a thong?

Yes, within 14 days, if it hasn't been worn and the hygiene seal is intact.

Which payment methods do you accept?

At launch: Bancontact, Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay and iDEAL. Payment is secure (HTTPS, 3D Secure).

Do I need an account?

No. You can order without one. Bo's Club is linked to your email address.

Bo and the brand

Who's Bo?

Bo is the brand's teddy bear. He models every new thong instead of a human model. You can adopt him (€49) and dress him every season with a €3 mini thong.

Is Bo a children's toy?

No. Bo is a collectible bear and a funny gift for adults, 18 and over.

Why 'unisex'?

Because we sort our thongs by cut and size, not by gender. Pick what fits your body. That's it.
