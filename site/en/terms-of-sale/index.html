1. Who is the seller?

The site is operated by UNIS SRL, whose details are given in the legal notice. If you have any questions, use the Contact page.

2. Scope

These terms apply to every order placed on the site by a consumer, that is, a person acting for private purposes. You accept them by ticking the box provided before confirming your order. The applicable version is the one online at the time of the order; you can save or print it.

Creating an account is never required to place an order.

3. The products

The main characteristics of each product (cut, sizes, composition, price) are shown on its product page. The thongs are made of 95% cotton and 5% elastane, certified OEKO-TEX Standard 100.

Bo is a collectible intended for people aged 14 and over. He is not designed as a toy for children; he is nevertheless tested against the European toy safety standard EN 71 and bears the CE marking.

Seasonal drops are produced in limited quantities and are never reissued. They are sold while stocks last.

4. Prices

Prices are shown in euros, including all taxes, with the VAT of the country of delivery (European Union OSS scheme). Delivery costs €3.90 per order and is free for purchases of €30 or more; it is displayed before you confirm.

For any price reduction, the reference price shown is the lowest price applied during the 30 days before the reduction (art. VI.18 of the Code of Economic Law, CEL).

In the event of an obvious pricing error, UNIS may cancel the order and refund all amounts paid in full.

5. Ordering

Ordering takes four steps: choosing products, checking the basket and costs, accepting these terms, and paying. The contract is concluded when you receive the confirmation email summarising the order.

6. Payment

Accepted methods: Bancontact, Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay and iDEAL, through a certified payment service provider. Payment is secure (HTTPS, 3D Secure); UNIS never has access to your bank details.

The products remain the property of UNIS until full payment.

7. Delivery

Delivery to European Union countries, normally within 2 to 4 working days of dispatch and no later than 30 days after the order (art. VI.43 CEL). Thongs are sent in a letterbox-sized pouch; Bo in his gift box.

The risk of loss or damage passes to you when you receive the parcel (art. VI.44 CEL). If the parcel arrives damaged, let us know as soon as possible with a photo.

8. Right of withdrawal

You have 14 calendar days from receipt of your order to withdraw from the contract, without giving any reason (art. VI.47 CEL). To exercise this right, inform us by a clear statement (Contact page) or using the model withdrawal form below, then send the products back no later than 14 days after informing us.

We will refund all amounts paid, including standard delivery costs, no later than 14 days after being informed of your withdrawal, using the same means of payment (art. VI.49 CEL). We may withhold the refund until we have received the products. Return shipping costs are borne by you.

Hygiene exception: for reasons of health protection and hygiene, the right of withdrawal does not apply to thongs and mini thongs whose hygiene label has been removed or which have been worn after delivery (art. VI.53, 5° CEL). An unworn thong with its label intact can be returned as normal. Bo can be returned if he is complete and in his box.

You may be held liable for any diminished value of the products resulting from handling other than what is necessary to establish their nature, characteristics and functioning.

9. Legal guarantee of conformity

All products are covered by the two-year legal guarantee of conformity from delivery (former Civil Code, art. 1649bis et seq.). In the event of a defect, you are entitled to repair or replacement of the product or, failing that, to a price reduction or a refund. Report the defect to us via the Contact page.

10. Liability

UNIS is not liable for damage resulting from misuse of the products or from force majeure. Nothing in these terms limits the rights the law guarantees you as a consumer.

The customer account is optional and free. Bo's Club (stamps and rewards) is described on its own page; its rules may change, without affecting benefits already earned. You can delete your account at any time from ‘My account’.

12. Personal data

The processing of your data is described in the privacy policy and on the Cookies page.
