Unisex thong: why the cut matters more than gender

In most shops, underwear starts with a question: men's or women's? At UNIS, we ask a different one: what fits your body?

Published on 3 February 2027

Two cuts, not two genders

Flat, with a smooth, close-fitting front. Pouch, with a shaped front that gives room without squeezing. Everyone picks according to their body and mood, not a label.

Eight sizes, from XS to 4XL

Your hip measurement is all you need to find your size. No 'men's' and 'women's' charts to cross-reference.

The same comfort for every body

The same wide, soft waistband, the same OEKO-TEX certified cotton, the same price: €5.

So where does Bo fit in?

Bo doesn't ask who the thong is for: he wears it first, every season. It's our way of showing the product without retouching a single body.
