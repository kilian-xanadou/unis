How to choose your thong size in 3 steps

Too small and it pinches, too big and it sags. Good news: all you need is a tape measure and 30 seconds.

Published on 6 January 2027

1. Measure your hips

Take a soft tape measure and wrap it around your hips at the widest point, without pulling it tight. Note the number in centimetres.

2. Read the chart

Check the size guide: from XS (82–88 cm) to 4XL (128–136 cm). Between two sizes? Go for the bigger one: exchanges are free anyway.

3. Pick your cut

Flat, with a smooth front, for a clean line under your clothes. Pouch, with a shaped front, for more room. Your size stays the same in both cuts.
