UNIS is the €5 unisex thong, from XS to 4XL, and Bo, the bear who wears it first. A new print every season, never brought back.

Our manifesto

Underwear shouldn't ask you who you are. We don't have a men's or women's section: we have two cuts, Flat or Pouch, and eight sizes.

Underwear shouldn't cost a fortune either. A soft, OEKO-TEX certified thong costs €5. Full stop.

And we'd rather make you laugh than make you self-conscious. No retouched models: Bo, our bear, wears every new print before you do.

Our values

Inclusion

A cut and a size, never a gender.

Honest pricing
