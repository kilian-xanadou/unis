UNIS puts fun in your underwear drawer: unisex thongs at €5, from XS to 4XL, made in Europe, and Bo, the bear who wears them first.

Why UNIS exists

We exist to put fun where there's never any: in an underwear drawer.

We believe a shared laugh is worth more than an expensive gift. That underwear can be a joke, a wink or a memory. And that everyone deserves that fun: every body, every gender, every size. Even bears.

We're here to laugh. And to get everyone laughing with us.

How we do it

Bo steals the show

A teddy bear poses instead of a model. It's funny before the box is even open, and no 'ideal' body is imposed.

Truly unisex

Two cuts, Flat or Pouch, and eight sizes from XS to 4XL. Never 'for men' or 'for women'.

Drops, not a catalogue

A new print every season, never reissued. Every drop is a reason to give, share and collect.

Fun at a joke price

€5 a thong: cheap enough to dare to give, good enough to wear. No fake sales.

Made in Europe

Made in the European Union, with OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certified cotton.

Donations with proof

When we support a cause, we show the amount and the charity, and publish the total with proof.

Who's behind UNIS?

UNIS is a student e-business project in Belgium. The team's faces will arrive with the site's photos.

What we sell

Our two red lines

  • Children: no children, no children's settings, no targeting of minors, and Bo is never linked to childhood.
  • Borderline: nothing crude, explicit, humiliating or mocking towards a body or a group. If in doubt, it's a no.

Community rules

  • We answer every question, even the funniest ones, within 24 hours on weekdays.
  • Criticism stays up: it helps us do better.
  • We remove insults, hate speech, spam and anything that sexualises Bo or links him to children.
  • Sharing a photo with #BoPorte? We'll only repost it with your permission.
