Frequently asked questions

Will the Christmas drop come back?

No. Every drop is a limited run and never comes back. Once it sells out, this page stays online and points you to the next drop.

What if the size is wrong?

Size exchanges are free within 14 days, if the thong hasn't been worn and the hygiene seal is intact.

When should I order for delivery before Christmas?

By Thursday 17 December at the latest: delivery takes 2 to 4 working days in the EU.
