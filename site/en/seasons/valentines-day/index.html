Frequently asked questions

Will the Valentine's drop come back?

No. Every drop is a limited run and never comes back. Once it sells out, this page stays online and points you to the next drop.

What if the size is wrong?

You can return it within 14 days if it hasn't been worn and the hygiene seal is intact, then order the right size.
