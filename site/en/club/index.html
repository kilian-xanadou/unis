Bo's Club: collect the seasons

Free and automatic from your first order. No account to create: your stamp card is linked to your email address.

How it works

  1. 1

    1 drop = 1 stamp

    Buy at least one item from a season and you get a stamp on your card.

  2. 3

    3 stamps

    A free mini thong for Bo with your next order.

  3. 6

    6 stamps

    All six seasons of a year: next edition's Bo at half price and your collector's certificate.

And there's more

  • Every drop 24 hours before everyone else.
  • Have your say: vote for one of next year's prints.
  • A little note from Bo on your birthday, with free shipping.

The full rules will be published at launch. Demo version: no stamps are saved.

The next drop, 24 hours before everyone

Sign up: one email a month, with the new print, Bo's limited editions and last delivery dates before the holidays.

You'll get an email to confirm your sign-up (double opt-in). Every email has an unsubscribe link. Your address is stored in our secure database, hosted in the European Union.
