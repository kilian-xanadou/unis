Gift boxes and packs: Bo, the Duo and the whole gang

Want a gift that's ready to give, without putting anything together yourself? Here's everything that ships already assembled.

Published on 16 November 2026

The gift box, €55

It's the complete present: the Christmas gift box brings together Bo Christmas edition, our 35 cm collectible bear, his numbered certificate and the Disco reindeer matching Duo, that is an adult thong and a mini thong for Bo in the same print. All in a gift box. At €55, shipping is free.

Who is it for?

For your other half, on Valentine's Day or for a birthday. For a friend who already collects Bo's editions. For someone who matters, when Secret Santa just isn't enough. Bo is a collectible for adults, 18 and over: he sits on a shelf and makes everyone who walks past smile.

Bo on his own, €49

Prefer to start simple? Bo, the UNIS bear arrives in his gift box with a mini thong from the current drop, his numbered certificate and a gift card. Bo Christmas edition also wears the season's accessory, in 500 numbered pieces.

Ready-to-give packs

On the Christmas drop page, two packs go into your bag in one click, at the normal price: the Secret Santa kit (3 matching Duos, €21) and the gang kit (the 5-pack of Essentials and 2 thongs from the drop, €30, free shipping). You choose one size for the whole pack.

The little extra

The matching Duo at €7 and the mini thong for Bo at €3 slip in alongside another present, or inside a card. The mini thong is one size: no size to guess.

Shipping

Thongs arrive in a kraft pouch that fits through the letterbox, Bo in his gift box. Delivery in 2 to 4 working days within the EU, €3.90, free from €30. For Christmas, order by Thursday 17 December at the latest.
