Cookies

This demo site doesn't set any analytics or advertising cookies.

It only keeps your bag in your browser's local storage (key 'unis-cart'), so it follows you from page to page. This data never leaves your device. You can delete it by clearing the site data in your browser.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which sends your IP address to Google.

At the real launch, a banner will ask for your consent before any analytics (Google Analytics) or advertising pixel (Meta), in line with the GDPR.
