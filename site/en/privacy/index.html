Privacy

Demonstration site for a student project: UNIS SRL is not a registered company and no sale is concluded on this site. The identifiers below are fictitious. Accounts, the waiting list, contact messages and cookie choices, however, are really stored.

Version of 16 November 2026

1. Data controller

UNIS SRL, whose details are given in the legal notice, is the controller of your personal data. For any question or to exercise your rights: Contact page.

2. Data collected, purposes and legal bases

  • Customer account: email, password (encrypted, never readable by UNIS), first name, language, preferred cut and size, Bo's Club stamps. Purpose: managing your account and the loyalty programme. Basis: performance of the contract (art. 6.1.b GDPR).
  • Newsletter and waiting list: email, language, preferred cut, date of consent. Purpose: telling you about drops and sending you the newsletter. Basis: your consent (art. 6.1.a), which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Contact messages: first name, email, subject, order number, message. Purpose: answering your request. Basis: our legitimate interest in replying to people who write to us and, where applicable, performance of the contract (art. 6.1.b and f).
  • Cookie choices: random identifier, choices made, date, language. Purpose: proving your consent. Basis: legal obligation (art. 6.1.c and art. 7.1 GDPR).
  • Orders (at launch): identity, addresses, products purchased. Purpose: fulfilling the order and meeting our accounting and tax obligations. Basis: contract and legal obligation.

We never sell your data and make no automated decisions about you.

3. Recipients and processors

  • Supabase (database and authentication), servers located in the European Union.
  • Render (site hosting), which processes the connection IP address; Google (fonts via Google Fonts). These providers may transfer data outside the European Union; such transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.
  • At launch: payment service provider, carrier and emailing tool, bound by a data processing agreement (art. 28 GDPR).

4. Retention periods

  • Account: until it is deleted, or after 3 years without logging in.
  • Waiting list and newsletter: until you unsubscribe.
  • Contact messages: 2 years after the last exchange.
  • Proof of cookie choices: 13 months.
  • Order data: for the legal retention period for accounting records.

5. Your rights

You can request access to your data, its rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and portability, and object to processing based on our legitimate interest (art. 15 to 21 GDPR). You can withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out.

You can delete your account and all its data yourself in ‘My account’. For other requests, write to us via the Contact page: we reply within one month.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).

6. Security

Encrypted connection (HTTPS), encrypted passwords, data access restricted by row-level security rules in the database (each person sees only their own account), no public read access to sign-up lists and messages.

7. Minors

Creating an account is reserved for people aged 16 and over. In Belgium, consent to the newsletter by a minor under 13 requires a parent's authorisation (Act of 30 July 2018, art. 7).

8. Cookies

The cookies and local storage used are detailed on the Cookies page, where you can change your choice at any time.
