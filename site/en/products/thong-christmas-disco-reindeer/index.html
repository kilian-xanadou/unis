What you need to know

Disco reindeer is a print from the Christmas drop: a limited run, never reissued. Bo wore it first, now it's your turn. Or the turn of the person whose name you drew.

Truly unisex: pick your cut, Flat (smooth front, clean line) or Pouch (shaped front, more room), then your size from your hip measurement, XS to 4XL. Wide, soft waistband, fine back strap, no itchy seams.

At €5, it's the gift that gets a laugh without emptying the budget, and that actually gets worn afterwards. Add the matching mini thong for Bo, or go straight for the €7 Duo.

Shipping and returns

Delivery across the European Union in 2 to 4 working days. €3.90, free from €30. Thongs arrive in a kraft pouch that fits through the letterbox, Bo in his gift box.

You have 14 days to return an item. For hygiene reasons, a thong can only be returned if it hasn't been worn and the hygiene seal is intact.
