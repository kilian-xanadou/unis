What you need to know

The Duo is the same Disco reindeer print in two sizes: a thong for you (or for whoever you're giving it to) and a mini thong for Bo. Matching, at last.

Choose the adult thong in a Flat or Pouch cut, from XS to 4XL. The mini thong is one size, made to fit Bo. All for €7, in a Christmas limited run.

It's the funniest Secret Santa gift to unwrap: one laugh when it's opened, a second one picturing Bo in his version. No Bo yet? That's an excellent reason to adopt him.

Shipping and returns

Delivery across the European Union in 2 to 4 working days. €3.90, free from €30. Thongs arrive in a kraft pouch that fits through the letterbox, Bo in his gift box.
