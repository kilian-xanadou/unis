What you need to know

Five Essential thongs in the same size, in mixed colours, for €20: €4 a thong. To fill your drawer, or kit out the whole gang.

Shipping and returns

Delivery across the European Union in 2 to 4 working days. €3.90, free from €30. Thongs arrive in a kraft pouch that fits through the letterbox, Bo in his gift box.
