What you need to know

Bo is our model, our muse and our mascot: a 35 cm honey-coloured teddy bear with a bow in the colour of the season. He wears every new thong before anyone else, and he never complains about the lighting.

In his box: Bo, a mini thong from the current drop, a numbered certificate and a gift card. He is a collectible for adults, 18 and over, not a toy. He sits on a shelf, a desk or a bedside table, and makes everyone who walks past smile.

You adopt him once, then dress him every season with a €3 mini thong. Or go for the €7 Duo and wear the same print as him.

Composition

35 cm collectible plush, honey-coloured, with a bow in the colour of the season. Delivered in his gift box with a numbered certificate. A collectible for adults, 18 and over, not a toy.

Shipping and returns

Delivery across the European Union in 2 to 4 working days. €3.90, free from €30. Thongs arrive in a kraft pouch that fits through the letterbox, Bo in his gift box.

You have 14 days to return an item. For hygiene reasons, a thong can only be returned if it hasn't been worn and the hygiene seal is intact.
