Frequently asked questions

Who is Bo?

Bo is the brand's teddy bear. He wears every new thong instead of a model. You can adopt him (€49) and dress him every season with a €3 mini thong.

Is Bo a children's toy?

No. Bo is a collectible and a funny gift for ages 14 and up. He still passes EU toy safety tests.

What does unisex thong mean?

Our thongs are sorted by cut, not gender: Flat, with a smooth front, or Pouch, with a shaped front that gives more room.

How do I choose my size?

Measure your hips at the widest point, then check the size guide, from XS (82–88 cm) to 4XL (128–136 cm).

Shipping and returns?

EU-wide delivery in 2 to 4 working days: €3.90, free from €30. Returns within 14 days if the thong hasn't been worn and the hygiene seal is intact.
