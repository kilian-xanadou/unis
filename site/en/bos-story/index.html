Bo's story: the bear who wears our thongs

Bo is our model, our face and our mascot. A honey-coloured collectible bear who puts on every new print before anyone else. Here's where he comes from.

A bear instead of a model

UNIS was born in 2026 from an idea by e-business students in Belgium: selling truly unisex thongs for €5. One awkward question remained: who do you show a thong on without imposing an 'ideal' body?

The answer came with a laugh. No retouched model, no perfect body: a teddy bear. He wears the thong, everyone gets the cut, and nobody compares themselves to him. Bo was born.

yes, I've tried on every print

Why a bear?

Because a bear is soft, and so are our thongs. Because a bear in a thong is funny before the box is even open. And because Bo has no gender and no clothing size: he shows that you choose underwear by cut, Flat or Pouch, not by a 'men' or 'women' box.

Bo is 35 cm tall, with honey-coloured fur, two black button eyes and no eyebrows (he insists). Every season he gets a new bow: red at Christmas, lilac in spring, raspberry for Pride.

my favourite? The next one.

Bo's wardrobe

Six times a year, Bo puts on a new print, in a limited run that is never reissued: Valentine's Day, Spring, Pride, Summer, Halloween and Christmas. In October he also wears the pink ribbon of the Pink October capsule. You can see his whole wardrobe on the seasons page.

Every print comes in three versions: the adult thong at €5, the mini thong for Bo at €3, and both together in the matching Duo at €7. That's how you end up dressed like your bear.

Bo, a user's guide

Bo is funny, a little cheeky, never crude. He speaks in the first person on our socials, has an opinion on everything (especially ugly sweaters), and replies to photos shared with #BoPorte.

His two rules: we never make fun of anyone's body, and we laugh with everyone. If a joke makes someone uncomfortable, Bo doesn't make it.

Adopting Bo

Bo is a collectible bear for adults, 18 and over. He comes in his gift box, with a mini thong from the current drop and a numbered certificate. Price: €49. You can adopt him here, or give him in a gift box with the season's Duo (€55).

Once he's adopted, all he needs is a €3 mini thong each season to get a new outfit. Looking for an idea for someone? Have a look at our gift ideas. Members of Bo's Club hear about every new drop 24 hours early.

Questions about Bo

Who is Bo?

Bo is the UNIS collectible bear. He wears every new thong before anyone else, instead of a model.

How much is Bo?

Why does Bo wear thongs?

To show our cuts without imposing an ideal body, and because it's funny. Bo proves you choose a thong by cut, not by gender.
