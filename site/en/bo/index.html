Bo, the teddy bear who wears our thongs

Bo is our model. No retouched photos, no ideal body: every new thong is first worn by a 35 cm honey-coloured bear, with a bow in the colour of the season.

Adopt him once (€49), then dress him for every drop with a €3 mini thong, or with the €7 Duo to wear the same print as him. The funny gift that makes people laugh without making anyone uncomfortable.

What's in the box

  • Bo, 35 cm, honey-coloured, bow in the colour of the season
  • A mini thong from the current drop
  • A lilac gift box with a window
  • A numbered certificate and a gift card

A collectible, not a toy

Bo is meant for ages 14 and up. He is still tested to the European toy safety standard EN 71 and carries the CE marking.
