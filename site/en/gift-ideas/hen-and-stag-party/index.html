Hen or stag do gift: the whole gang in UNIS, €4 a head

A great hen or stag do means a tight-knit gang, legendary photos and nobody left on the sidelines.

Published on 16 November 2026

The organiser's headache

Custom T-shirts that cost too much, accessories that break before midnight, ideas that humiliate the person you're celebrating: organising a hen or stag do means juggling the budget, the sizes and everyone's sensitivities. You need a shared in-joke that's cheap and makes everyone laugh, starting with the star of the weekend.

€4 a head with the 5-pack

The 5-pack of Essential thongs costs €20, so €4 per person. Mixed colours, OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certified cotton, made in Europe. The pack comes in a single size: for a gang with a mix of sizes, combine several packs or add Essential thongs at €5 each, everyone in their own size. With two packs, you go over €30 and shipping is free.

From XS to 4XL: nobody left on the sidelines

Our thongs are truly unisex: two cuts, Flat or Pouch, and eight sizes from XS to 4XL, based on hip measurement. No gendered aisles to wander, no size you can't find. Drop the size guide in the group chat and let everyone measure themselves in 30 seconds.

Bo comes along for the weekend

Every good gang needs a mascot. Bo, the UNIS bear, a 35 cm collectible plush, photobombs every picture, and he already has his outfit. At €49, split between ten, that's under €5 each. And afterwards he stays with the person you're celebrating, as a souvenir of the weekend.

Funny, never humiliating

The golden rule: you laugh with the person you're celebrating, never at them. A matching thong for the whole gang is a collective in-joke: everyone plays along, nobody gets put on the spot against their will. And unlike party props, it keeps getting worn long after the weekend.

Fancy a print?

The current drop gives you a ready-made theme: the Disco reindeer thong at €5, in a limited run. Is your weekend planned for the summer? The summer drop comes out on 15 June 2027, in bright colours.
