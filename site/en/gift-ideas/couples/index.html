Valentine's Day gift for couples: all three of you, matching

Flowers and chocolates, been there. This year, give the gift that makes them laugh, then blush.

Published on 16 November 2026

The gift that makes them laugh, then blush

Valentine's Day has a problem: everything looks the same. Flowers wilt, chocolates vanish in three days, and classic lingerie piles on the pressure: the size, the body, the expectations. A UNIS thong is a wink first and foremost. You laugh, because Bo is wearing the same one. As for what happens next, we'll leave that to the two of you.

Matching, whatever your gender

Our thongs are truly unisex. No his-and-hers versions: two cuts, Flat or Pouch, and eight sizes, from XS to 4XL. Same-gender couple, different genders or outside the boxes altogether: you can both wear the same print, each in your own cut and size. Bo too, in his.

From a cheeky wink to a big present

For a cheeky wink: the matching Duo at €7, an adult thong and a mini thong for Bo in the same print. To match each other: two drop thongs at €5 each, plus the mini thong for Bo if you've already adopted him. For a gift that lasts: the gift box at €55, with limited-edition Bo, his numbered certificate and the matching Duo. Free shipping.

The Valentine's drop lands on 3 February 2027

Embroidered hearts and poetic pink: the Valentine's Day drop comes out on 3 February 2027, in a limited run, with Bo Heart edition in 500 numbered pieces. Plenty of time for delivery well before 14 February. Sign up on the Valentine's Day page to get access 24 hours before everyone else. Want to give something right now? The Christmas drop products and Bo, the UNIS bear are available today.

The size, without spoiling the surprise

Giving underwear without knowing the exact size is stressful. Have a discreet look at the size of something your other half already wears, then compare it with our size guide, based on hip measurement. If they're between two sizes, go for the larger one. And if it doesn't fit, you can return it within 14 days, as long as the thong hasn't been worn and the hygiene seal is intact.

A keepsake that doesn't wilt

Bo, on the other hand, doesn't wilt. With his numbered certificate, he becomes the witness of your Valentine's Day, and he changes outfit every season, for €3 a mini thong. In ten years' time, you'll still know which year he arrived.
