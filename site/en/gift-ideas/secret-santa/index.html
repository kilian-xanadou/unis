Funny Secret Santa gift: the €5 thong that gets the whole table laughing

You've drawn a name, you've got €10 and zero ideas. Good news: Bo is already wearing the answer.

Published on 16 November 2026

The Secret Santa problem

A €10 budget, a person you barely know, and a whole table waiting for the unwrapping. Plastic gadgets end up in the bin the next day, and heavy-handed jokes make everyone squirm. You need a gift that's funny but not crude. A gift someone can open in front of their boss, and actually wear afterwards.

Why a UNIS thong works every time

Because it's Bo, our collectible bear, who wears it in the photo: the giggles start before the pouch is even open. Because it's truly unisex: no gendered sections, just a cut, Flat or Pouch, and a size from XS to 4XL. And because at €5, there's money left for the card. The cotton is OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certified and everything is made in Europe: funny, yes; disposable, no.

Three ideas for your budget

For €3: the mini thong for Bo, perfect if your person has already adopted Bo. For €5: the Disco reindeer thong, a print from the Christmas drop, in a limited run. For €7: the matching Duo, an adult thong and a mini thong for Bo in the same print. It's the funniest to unwrap, and it fits a €10 budget.

What if you don't know their size?

That's the Secret Santa trick question. First solution: the mini thong for Bo, in one size, no hip measurement to guess. Second solution: the adult thong, with our size guide in front of you; if they're between two sizes, go for the larger one. If the size isn't right, the thong can be returned within 14 days, provided it hasn't been worn and the hygiene seal is intact.

Order in time for Christmas

We deliver in 2 to 4 working days across the European Union. To get your parcel before Christmas, order by Thursday 17 December at the latest. Shipping costs €3.90 and is free from €30: if the whole office is doing Secret Santa, put your orders together. With the 5-pack of Essentials at €20 and two thongs from the drop, you pass the threshold and nobody pays for shipping.

After the party

The best moment often comes the next day: the photo of the gift, sitting next to Bo, doing the rounds in the group chat. For more ideas on the same budget, also read our 4 Secret Santa gift ideas under €10.
